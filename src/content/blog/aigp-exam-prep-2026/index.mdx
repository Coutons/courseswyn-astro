## What Is the IAPP AIGP Certification?

The **IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP)** certification is designed for professionals responsible for developing, deploying, and managing AI systems safely, ethically, and in compliance with applicable laws. IAPP describes the credential as evidence of competency in AI development, ethical AI deployment, and AI management practices that support safety and trust.

The current AIGP Body of Knowledge version 2.1 became effective on **February 2, 2026**. It organizes the certification into four domains and 13 competencies, with performance indicators that define what candidates need to know for the exam.

### AIGP Exam Domains

| AIGP Domain | What You Need to Understand |
|---|---|
| **Domain I: Foundations of AI Governance** | What AI is, why AI needs governance, organizational expectations, policies, and procedures across the AI lifecycle. |
| **Domain II: Laws, Standards & Frameworks** | Data privacy laws, other applicable laws, the EU AI Act, industry standards, and AI governance tools. |
| **Domain III: Governing AI Development** | Governing AI model design, building, training, testing, release, monitoring, and maintenance. |
| **Domain IV: Governing AI Deployment & Use** | Deployment decisions, AI model assessment, risk reduction, ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and accountability. |

A strong AIGP course should therefore do more than define AI terms. It should help you apply governance controls to real AI development and deployment decisions, including data governance, model assessment, vendor oversight, monitoring, and compliance.

## How to Pass the IAPP AIGP Exam

### 1. Study by domain, not by topic

The AIGP exam is organized around four domains: foundations, laws and frameworks, AI development governance, and AI deployment governance. Studying domain by domain helps you understand how concepts connect across the AI lifecycle.

### 2. Prioritize the EU AI Act and AI risk management

The EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and NIST AI RMF appear repeatedly across AIGP-focused courses because they are central to modern AI governance programs.

### 3. Learn governance artifacts

Know how to use model cards, AI policies, impact assessments, risk registers, monitoring plans, and vendor governance documents. These artifacts translate governance theory into operational controls.

### 4. Use official IAPP materials

IAPP provides an AIGP Body of Knowledge, exam blueprint, study guide, and sample questions. These should be your baseline resources, with Udemy courses used to reinforce and explain the material.

### 5. Practice applied scenarios

The AIGP exam assesses whether you can apply governance knowledge to AI development and deployment situations. Focus on scenario-based reasoning: identify the risk, determine the applicable framework, and select the appropriate governance control.
