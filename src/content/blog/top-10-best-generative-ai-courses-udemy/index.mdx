## What is RAG and why is it still the core GenAI skill?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation connects a language model to an external knowledge base at answer time. Instead of relying only on what was baked into the model's weights, a RAG system embeds your documents, stores them in a vector database, retrieves the passages most similar to the question, and passes them to the model as context before it writes an answer.

That solves the two problems that block enterprise adoption: hallucination, because answers are grounded in retrieved sources, and staleness, because you can update the document store without retraining the model. In 2027 the pattern has matured rather than disappeared — the interesting work has moved to **agentic RAG**, where the agent decides what to retrieve, when to re-query, and how to verify what came back, plus evaluation and observability so you can prove the system improved.

Courses #4, #5, #6, #7 and #8 in this ranking all include substantial RAG work. If RAG is your main goal, our [RAG course comparison](/blog/best-rag-courses-on-udemy/) goes deeper, and the [LangChain guide](/blog/top-10-best-langchain-courses-udemy/) covers the framework side.
