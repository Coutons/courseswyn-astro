## What is The Complete AI Curriculum in 2027?

The Complete AI Curriculum is Ed Donner's eight-course training roadmap on Udemy. Donner is the co-founder and CTO of Nebula.io, a former JPMorgan managing director, and the instructor behind courses with more than a million enrolments. His [curriculum page](https://edwarddonner.com/curriculum/) describes the eight courses as "one roadmap" and groups them into two programs.

**The Established AI Engineer Program** is the original six courses. Donner says they "complement each other and can be taken in any order", but the order shown is the most natural progression. There are two official entry points:

- **Use products to build agents:** start with AI Builder and create agents in n8n.
- **Use agents to build products:** start with AI Coder and build software with Claude Code.

**The Flagship AI-Native Program** is the newer pair: AI SDLC and AI FDE. Donner describes it as "my new program to position you for the agentic era, closely aligned with industry needs." These are advanced, role-focused courses rather than replacements for the six.

**The destination** is the [Proficient AI Engineer directory](https://edwarddonner.com/proficient/): complete all six established milestones, build and deploy real projects, and you can apply. Donner's AI FDE page adds an important exception: **AI FDE can take the place of the Production Track** for directory eligibility.

That is the verified structure. Anything beyond it in this guide — ratings, prices, hours, and who each course suits — is labelled as data or as our assessment, not as Donner's official sequence.

Donner's official order for the six established courses is AI Builder, AI Coder, AI Leader, Core Track, Agentic Track, Production Track.
