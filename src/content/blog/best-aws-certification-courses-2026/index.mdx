## AWS exam fees and vouchers: the cost nobody mentions

Two costs sit outside every price on this page, and most course guides skip them entirely. If you are budgeting for a certification, this is the part you need.

### What the AWS exam actually costs

Every course above is preparation. The exam itself is booked through Pearson VUE and paid to AWS at a fixed list price:

| AWS exam level | Certifications | Exam fee |
|----------------|----------------|----------|
| Foundational | Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02), AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) | $100 |
| Associate | Solutions Architect, Developer, SysOps / CloudOps | $150 |
| Professional | Solutions Architect Pro, DevOps Engineer Pro | $300 |
| Specialty | Security, Advanced Networking, Machine Learning | $300 |

A realistic total for a first certification is therefore about **$11 for the course plus $100 to $150 for the exam** — roughly $111 to $161. That is the number to compare against bootcamps and degree programmes, which cost thousands for the same credential.

### Does Udemy sell AWS exam vouchers?

No, and this is the single most common misunderstanding we see. Udemy sells discounted prep courses. It does not resell AWS exam vouchers. If a page tells you a Udemy coupon covers your exam, it does not.

### Where AWS exam vouchers actually come from

Vouchers do exist, but not where most people look:

- **AWS bulk vouchers (organizations only).** AWS sells exam vouchers in bulk through Pearson VUE, with volume discounts. This route is for employers buying for teams, not for individuals.
- **AWS promo codes.** AWS occasionally releases discount codes for specific exams — a 50% off code for the AI Practitioner exam has circulated more than once. These are tied to a single exam and expire.
- **AWS Second Chance retake offers.** At certain points of the year AWS bundles a free retake with an exam purchase, which removes the cost risk of failing.
- **AWS training and community programmes.** AWS has run schemes where completing training milestones earns exam credit, including a $100 Cloud Practitioner voucher for students who work through AWS's badge programme.
- **Third-party resellers.** Sites such as getcertified4less sell vouchers below AWS list price. Worth checking, but compare against the $100 / $150 / $300 table above — a "discounted" voucher above face value is not a discount.

When any of these offers is live, we list it on our [AWS exam vouchers page](/udemy-aws-exam-vouchers) rather than here, so the guide above does not go stale the week after a promotion ends.

### The retake rule

If you fail, you wait 14 days before retaking and pay the exam fee again. There is no limit on retakes. Budget for one retake rather than assuming a single payment covers you — and note that the Second Chance offer exists precisely because AWS knows a meaningful share of candidates fail first time.

The practical takeaway: the cheapest route to an AWS certification is a coupon on a prep course plus one exam fee, bought at the right moment. Buying the course at full price, or paying a reseller more than face value for a voucher, only makes the same path more expensive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which AWS certification should I take first?

If you have no cloud experience, take **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)**. If you already work in IT, skip it and go straight to **Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03)** — it is the certification employers ask for most, and Cloud Practitioner adds little once you have associate-level skills.

### How much does an AWS certification cost in total?

Budget about **$11 for the Udemy prep course** with a coupon, plus the **AWS exam fee**: $100 for Foundational, $150 for Associate, and $300 for Professional or Specialty. A first Cloud Practitioner certification therefore lands around $111 all in; an Associate certification around $161.

### Does Udemy sell AWS exam vouchers?

**No.** Udemy sells discounted prep courses, not AWS exam vouchers. AWS itself only sells vouchers in bulk to organizations through Pearson VUE. Individuals get discounts through AWS promo codes, retake offers and third-party resellers instead — all listed on our [exam voucher page](/udemy-aws-exam-vouchers).

### Are AWS exam vouchers worth buying?

**Only if you find a genuine discount.** AWS vouchers are sold at face value to organizations, so a third-party voucher is worth it when it is priced below the AWS list price of $100, $150 or $300. Never pay more than the standard exam fee for one.

### How much does the Cloud Practitioner exam cost?

**$100 USD.** It is one of two Foundational-level AWS exams at that price; the other is the newer AWS Certified AI Practitioner.

### How much does the Solutions Architect Associate exam cost?

**$150 USD**, which is the standard price for all Associate-level AWS exams. Add roughly $11 for a discounted Udemy prep course and the total is about $161.

### Do I need IT experience before taking an AWS certification?

**Not for Cloud Practitioner**, which requires none. Associate-level certifications assume about a year of IT work, and professional-level exams expect two or more years of hands-on AWS experience.

### What happens if I fail an AWS exam?

You wait **14 days** before retaking it and pay the exam fee again. There is no limit on retakes. AWS runs a Second Chance retake offer at certain times of year that covers the second attempt, and we track it on our [exam voucher page](/udemy-aws-exam-vouchers).

### Are Udemy courses enough to pass an AWS exam?

**Yes, for the exam they target.** The courses on this page include practice exams. Add the free AWS documentation and hands-on time in a free-tier account and you are well covered.

### How long does it take to get AWS certified?

**Two to four weeks** for Cloud Practitioner, **six to ten weeks** for an associate certification, and **twelve to twenty weeks** for a professional one, assuming a few hours of study most days.

### Are AWS certifications worth it in 2026?

**Yes**, for most people moving into or up in cloud work. AWS still holds the largest share of the cloud market, and a certification is the cheapest signal you can show an employer — cheaper than a degree, faster than a bootcamp.
